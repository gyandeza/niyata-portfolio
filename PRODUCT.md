# Niyata PRODUCT.md

## Product
Niyata — professional digital home of Gilang Yandeza.

## Primary audiences

1. Senior professional / hiring stakeholders
2. Academic supervisors and research collaborators
3. Professional communities
4. Potential project, speaking and teaching collaborators

## What visitors should understand

Within 60 seconds:
- Gilang works at the intersection of operations, supply chain, digital transformation, project delivery and applied AI.
- The work is grounded in real operating problems rather than technology for its own sake.
- Selected projects demonstrate systems thinking, evidence discipline and practical delivery.

## What visitors should be able to do

- understand the professional positioning
- inspect selected work
- understand Gilang’s role and decision process
- interact with selected safe prototypes
- distinguish verified outcomes from illustrative concepts
- find research / teaching themes
- connect for a relevant professional reason

## Portfolio principles

- Evidence over marketing claims.
- Public-safe by default for enterprise work.
- Show decisions and constraints, not only final screens.
- Interactive demos must expose assumptions.
- Do not imply adoption, client endorsement or business impact without evidence.

## RouteRisk status

RouteRisk is presented as:
**Operational route decision prototype / Niyata Labs**

It is not currently presented as:
- validated safety system
- production dispatch authority
- B2B SaaS with proven customers
- source of quantified business impact

## RouteRisk users

Illustrative target users:
- dispatch coordinator
- logistics supervisor
- route / field planner
- operations manager

## RouteRisk task

Given a set of route conditions:
1. inspect the risk factors,
2. see the transparent weighted score,
3. understand the risk band,
4. receive a simple dispatch recommendation.

## RouteRisk design mode

Case-study narrative: **Experience**
Interactive calculator: **Operate**

## RouteRisk safety boundary

The interactive calculator is a demonstration model. It must state clearly that:
- factor values are illustrative,
- weights are a prototype decision model,
- results are not a replacement for field inspection, safety procedure, weather warnings or managerial approval.


## GILANG AI HQ status

GILANG AI HQ is presented as:
**Personal AI Operating System / system architecture prototype**

Public claims may include:
- modular agent architecture,
- task-contract pattern,
- workflow orchestration,
- approval / risk boundaries,
- tested foundation execution flow.

Do not claim:
- fully autonomous operation,
- production security certification,
- universal access to personal accounts,
- or measured productivity ROI without evidence.
